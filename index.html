Case Study 01 // Operations Automation

Multi-Year AI & Workflow Platform

Role: Program Manager (Legal Ops DRI) | Duration: October 2023 – Present

The Operational Challenge

Our org managed over 30,000 regulatory information requests per year coming in via highly fragmented channels (PDFs, paper mail, emails, screenshots). Triaging was entirely manual, taking critical SMEs bandwidth away from high-judgment issues. This manual drag led to severe operational bottleneck risks in overall speed and accuracy.

Phased Roadmap & Execution Strategy

Phase 1: Build the Foundation

Deployed Workato as our enterprise integration middleware, connecting 27 secure platform pathways. This proved our deterministic automation was reliable before introducing the variable logic of AI (which was still very early at the time).

Phase 2: Intelligent Routing

Integrated internal LLM gateways on the intake queue to parse, categorize, and auto-route requests to 17 downstream desks. I designed a human-in-the-loop feedback button to ensure continuous accuracy tracking.

Phase 3: Automated Processing

Engineered a prompt-chained, parameterized extraction workflow for high-volume third party requests to automate data retrieval directly against secure data environments, complete with audit trail logs.

Phase 4: Agentic Framework

Partnered directly with an engineering group to build an universal intake engine that puts this process into the rest of our AI ecosystem, to allow end users to build mroe custom tailed workflows for themselves.

Key Strategic Decisions & Tradeoffs

  • Middleware Procurement: Selected Workato over a solution that was more tailored for legal to minimize maintenance, technical debt, and budget impact, allowing our lean team to focus on our core work.
  • Safe Sequence: Implementing stable and auditable routing paths before deploying data extraction modules. This allowed operational partners to build full trust in the automated framework sequentially.

The Long-term Impact

Over 1,600 hours saved in production to date, with a verified roadmap driving over 5,750 hours of annual staff recovery across 8 separate stakeholder business units.

Case Study 04 // AI Procurement

AI Redlining for High-Volume Contracts

Role: Program DRI | Duration: Jan 2026 – Present

The Capacity Problem

Our organization faced an ambitious 70% expected growth in contracts to review with a strictly capped, flat internal team headcount. High-judgment commercial agreements required intensive mechanical, line-by-line comparison reviews. Prior attempts using general in-house tools failed because models continually replaced whole paragraphs rather than executing tracked-change, word-level surgical edits.

The Solution

I structured this as an operational resource and tool vetting challenge. I wrote the detailed job specifications, hired, and now direct an AI Legal Ops Contractor to serve as my primary technical resource on the build.

To bypass marketing demos, I engineered a **"Gold Standard Test"** benchmark: I anonymized a live contract and playbooks, ran 10 counterparty drafts against 6 vendor models, and blindly graded their output against our legal experts' actual negotiation edits. We killed our initial pilot due to the discovered limits, and selected 2 vendor finalists for parallel in-production sandboxes.

Projected Impact

Targeting 1000+ professional staff hours saved annually. Also secured $61K in immediate operational hiring savings by sourcing a highly qualified specialist resource under budgeted scope.

Case Study 05 // Data & Metrics

Analytics Infrastructure (Snowflake & Looker)

The Operational Landscape

Despite managing $$$ (millions) in annual external services across multiple operational channels, legal leadership had no consolidated data on invoice cycle times, operational bottlenecks, vendor concentrations, or general spend metrics for their teams.

Designing the Stack

I defined and led the data requirements, partnering with Data Engineering to land the spend platform data directly inside Snowflake. Data Eng mapped and modeled the schema joins, then ** I personally designed and configured the production dashboards in Looker**. These included:

  • Platform Health Dashboard: Tracked operational metrics, invoicing errors, and automated routing health.
  • CLO Dashboard: Surfaced high-level spend analysis and vendor concentration metrics.
  • Team Specific Suite: Provided department heads with their matter level spend in real time.

The Outcome

Successfully replaced opinion-driven decision-making with objective, clean data pipelines. This dataset became the foundational baseline that proved the $5.0M business case for the subsequent Brightflag platform migration.
