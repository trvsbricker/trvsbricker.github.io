Core Architectural Principles

01 / INSTRUMENT BEFORE OPTIMIZING

Without reliable metrics, faster execution simply compounds chaos. I prioritize mapping data schemas, designing telemetry, and establishing clear operational baselines before introducing structural changes or AI components.

02 / DE-RISK BY SEQUENCING RISK CLASSES

In regulated, high-stakes environments, a "big bang" rollout creates critical vulnerabilities. I introduce automated routing first (low risk), structured extraction second (medium risk), and fully autonomous orchestration only after data pathways are battle-tested.

03 / TRUST SYSTEM-LEVEL OWNERSHIP

Operating cadences, enterprise platforms, and automation loops fail when nobody owns the system end-to-end. I bridge the technical and business gap by acting as the single accountable owner on major transformations.

Multi-Year AI & Workflow Platform

Role: Program Manager (Sole Tech Operations DRI) | Duration: October 2023 – Present

/ The Challenge

Managing over 50,000 regulatory information requests per year arriving in deeply fragmented modalities (PDFs, paper mail, emails, screenshots). Triaging was highly manual, taking key SME bandwidth away from high-judgment decisions, leading to severe operational risks in case processing speed and accuracy.

/ Phased Architecture & Execution

PHASE 1: Orchestration Foundation

Stood up Workato as the enterprise integration layer, connecting 27 platform connectors (IT, Security, Privacy paths). Proof of deterministic scale before implementing AI.

PHASE 2: AI-Assisted Routing

Interlinked custom LLM gateways directly on the email intake queue to parse, categorize, and route to 17 downstream desks with a unique one-click human feedback loop.

PHASE 3: Deterministic AI Workflow

Engineered a highly stable, prompt-chained, and parameterized extraction workflow for high-volume subpoenas to automate data retrieval directly against databases.

PHASE 4: Agentic Universal Intake

Pivoted to a central platform MCP server fronting an agentic workflow to dynamically select playbooks and tools in real time during NYC technical surge.

/ Key Strategic Decisions & Tradeoffs

  • Built vs. Bought Middleware: Evaluated MuleSoft and Workato. Selected Workato to minimize custom code and long-term tech debt, allocating engineering hours entirely to custom AI agents rather than integration pipe maintenance.
  • De-risked via Sequence: Chose to establish deterministic routing capabilities before deploying AI extraction modules. This strategy allowed operational teams to build confidence in the foundational automation framework.

/ The Impact

~1,600 hours saved already in production phases, with a verified roadmap driving over 5,750 hours saved annually across 8 stakeholder organizations.

Federated Customer Search Platform

Role: Platform Program Lead | Duration: Dec 2023 – Present

/ The Challenge

Executing high-volume customer records requests required internal analysts to navigate 14+ siloed administrative search tools and legacy databases. Search pipelines averaged 15 minutes per query, suffered from strict case-sensitivity errors, and lacked consolidated user data compliance controls.

/ Approach & Technical Decisions

Rather than designing a costly standalone legal application, I reframed the business requirement from "a dedicated legal tool" to a "unified platform data primitive." I partnered directly with the Engineering team to construct the federated search engine on top of their existing robust Compliance Risk Platform.

I authored the technical requirements and P/PS document to drive alignment across Compliance, Security, Data, and Legal. The platform normalized multiple irregular inputs (DOB, partial tax IDs, transaction hashes, wallet addresses) into an access-controlled, ranked-result user interface.

/ The Impact

Consolidated 14+ disparate admin legacy databases into a single interface. Saved over 1,000 engineering/SME hours within the first 12 months of deployment.

AI Redlining for High-Volume Contracts

Role: Program DRI | Duration: Oct 2025 – Present

/ The Capacity Problem

Our organization faced an ambitious 71% YoY institutional growth mandate with a strictly capped, flat legal team headcount. High-judgment commercial agreements required intensive mechanical, line-by-line comparison reviews. Prior attempts using in-house tooling failed because models continually replaced whole paragraphs rather than executing tracked-change, word-level surgical edits.

/ The Solution

I structured this as an operational resource and tool vetting challenge. I wrote the detailed job specifications, hired, and now direct an AI Legal Ops Contractor to serve as my primary technical resource on the build.

To bypass marketing demos, I engineered a **"Gold Standard Test"** benchmark: I anonymized a live contract and playbooks, ran 10 counterparty drafts against 6 vendor models, and blindly graded their output against our legal experts' actual negotiation edits. We killed our in-house tool pilot publicly due to programmatic limits, and selected 2 vendor finalists (Ivo & GC AI) for parallel in-production sandboxes.

/ Projected Impact

Targeting 660 to 1,300 attorney hours saved annually. Secured $61K in immediate operational hiring savings by sourcing a highly qualified technical resource under budgeted scope.
